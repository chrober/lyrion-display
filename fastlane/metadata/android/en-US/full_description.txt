Lyrion Display turns a Fire TV, Android TV device, tablet, or phone into a dedicated fullscreen Now Playing display for Lyrion Music Server.

Choose a Lyrion server and player once. On subsequent launches, the app goes straight to Material Skin's existing Now Playing screen. It displays playback information without controlling the player or requiring a cloud account.

REQUIREMENTS
- A running Lyrion Music Server (LMS) with the Material Skin plugin installed and enabled on the server.
- At least one Lyrion player available on that server.
- An Android 5.1 (API 22) or newer device with network access to the server.
- Authenticated Lyrion servers are not currently supported.

FEATURES
- Automatic local-network server discovery or manual server address entry.
- Remembered server and player selection for quick startup.
- Fullscreen album art and playback information from Material Skin.
- Optional progress display, technical track details, playlist position, and clock.
- Material Skin themes, color modes, and accent colors.
- TV remote and touchscreen support, plus an optional keep-screen-awake setting.
- No additional server plugin beyond Material Skin, no Google Play Services, and no cloud account.

Lyrion Display is an independent, unofficial open-source companion. Material Skin runs on your Lyrion server, while playback is controlled from your usual Lyrion clients. Local Lyrion servers commonly use HTTP; prefer a trusted network or HTTPS where available.
